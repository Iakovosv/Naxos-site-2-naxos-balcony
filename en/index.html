Welcome

Welcome to Naxos Balcony Apartments, your own retreat in the picturesque Glinado of Naxos. Our fully equipped rooms combine traditional Cycladic hospitality with modern comforts and panoramic views of the Aegean Sea. Our accommodation is located at a privileged, key point of the island, offering the ideal balance between tranquility and immediate access. Within just a few minutes, you can reach Naxos Town, as well as some of the island's most beautiful beaches. Enjoy an authentic holiday experience, away from the noise but close to everything.

Naxos & Glinado

Discover the magic of the island and the tranquility of our traditional village

Glinado Naxos

  • Traditional location: Just 5 minutes (5km) from the port and Naxos Town
  • Peace & Tranquility: Away from tourist noise, in the heart of Naxian nature
  • Beautiful view: Panoramic view of the Aegean Sea, Paros and surrounding islands.
  • Convenience: Within short distance you will find Mini Market, pharmacy and bus stop for your transportation.
  • Hub: The village's location allows quick access to both the coasts and mountainous Naxos.
  • Nearby beaches: Agios Prokopios (12'), Agia Anna (9'), Agios Georgios (11'), Alyko (18'), Mikri Vigla (20') and Hawaii (15')
  • Local taste: Authentic taverns and the village's traditional bakery with fresh local products

Naxos Attractions

  • Portara Portara - The iconic marble monument (12 minutes 5.5 km)
  • Naxos Castle Naxos Castle - Medieval town (12 minutes)
  • Naxos Town Naxos Town - Traditional alleys and shops (about 12 minutes)
  • Beaches: Plaka, Agios Prokopios, Agia Anna, Mikri Vigla, Alyko & Hawaii (10-20 minutes)
  • Mountainous Naxos: Apiranthos, Filoti, Koronos (40'), and Chalki (20') for the famous Kitro liqueur
  • Yria Archaeological Site: The impressive ancient temple of Dionysus, just 10 minutes away
  • The Kouros of Naxos: Visit the imposing ancient statues in Melanes (15') and Apollo (53')

Activities & Experiences

  • Watersports: windsurfing, kitesurfing, diving
  • Nature & Adventure: Mountain treks, hiking on ancient trails and mountain biking
  • Gastronomy: Tasting of local products, such as Naxos Graviera cheese and the renowned Potatoes
  • Wine tasting: Visit local wineries to taste the exquisite varieties of Naxian wine.
  • Traditional festivals: Experience authentic Naxian entertainment with violins and local dances.
  • Sunsets: Unique moments watching the sun disappear into the Aegean from our balcony
  • Sailing & Boat: Day trips by sailboat or speedboat to the Small Cyclades and remote beaches

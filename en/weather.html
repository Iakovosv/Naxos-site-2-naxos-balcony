Useful tips for our beaches

💨 Naxos Wind Guide: Best Beaches for Swimming
North Winds (Meltemi): When the station shows a N/NW wind, the beaches of Agios Prokopios and Agia Anna are the best choices, as they remain calm. South Winds: In case of South winds, we recommend visiting the beaches on the northern side of the island or Naxos Chora.
☀️ Sun Protection:
Check our UV Index. When the value is above 6, sunbathing at Naxos Balcony or the beach requires high protection.
